What do "common" and "rare" mean here?
Common
Reported on 60% or more of checklists that week. Expect to see or hear it on most visits.
Fairly common
Reported on 30–60% of checklists. Good odds, but not guaranteed.
Occasional
Reported on 10–30% of checklists. You might get lucky.
Uncommon
Reported on 3–10% of checklists. A nice find if you do.
Rare
Reported on under 3% of checklists. Genuinely unusual for this reserve.
Not recorded
No local records for this week yet. It could still be around, just hasn't been logged.

Who was Michael Eades?

Michael Eades (1954–2002), after whom this Reserve is dedicated, made a major contribution to a heightened awareness and understanding of the special and fragile natural environment of the Blue Mountains, which resulted in the recognition of environmentally sensitive land in Council's Land Environmental Plan 1991. Eades's work also contributed significantly to the eventual federal government listing, at a National level, of the Blue Mountains Sedge and Shrub swamps.

Eades was a champion of the need to protect the unique flora of the Mountains and identified a number of endangered plant species. He provided critical advice and support to community volunteer endeavours in bushland protection and the establishment of the Council's Bushcare Program.

Who built this bird app?

This bird app has been created by birder Katie Cordes. I am regularly seen walking the trails of Michael Eades Reserve, as I have been for nearly 20 years, with binos and camera around my neck. If you see me - say hi!

This app has been built to help visitors learn about the local birds in the reserve and encourage everyone to protect and preserve it. Please don't litter, and leave no trace.
